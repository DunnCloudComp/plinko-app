How it works

Your result is decided the moment you drop, by a secure random draw. Like online Plinko, every ball gets one fair 50/50 left-or-right choice per peg row from your browser's cryptographic random generator (crypto.getRandomValues). The number of "rights" is your slot, so the middle is common and the edges are rare.

The physics is the show. Balls fall with gravity, bounce off pegs and walls, and bump into each other. A gentle sideways guide (never up or down) makes each ball clip pegs on the side its draw says. If another ball knocks it off course, the guide steers it back, and in the last few rows balls stop colliding so each one drops cleanly into its slot.

Collisions are just for show. They can't change your result. The payout always uses the slot drawn when you dropped, and the ball lands there.

No queue. Every tap, click or Space drops a ball right away, and the bet is taken then. Up to 150 balls can be on the board at once. At that cap the Drop button pauses for a moment. Drop height (Settings) only changes how far balls fall before the pegs.

Same odds every way you play. Each ball is independent, so single drops, auto-drop and button mashing all have exactly the odds shown below. If you leave while balls are falling, they're paid out from their drawn slots on your next visit.

The top prize grows with the board (29× on 8 rows up to 130× on 16) because edges get rarer with every row. That keeps every board at about 97% with roughly two-thirds of the slots paying 1× or more. Coins are pretend and can't be bought or cashed out.
