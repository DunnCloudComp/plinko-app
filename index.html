How it works

Your result is decided the moment you drop, by a secure random draw. Like online Plinko, a board set to N rows gives every ball N fair 50/50 left-or-right choices from your browser's cryptographic random generator (crypto.getRandomValues). The number of "rights" is your slot (N+1 slots), so the middle is common and the edges are rare.

The physics is the show. Every board shows the same 16-row peg field. The Rows setting only changes how many win slots there are and how wide they are. Balls fall with gravity, bounce off pegs and walls, and bump into each other. A gentle sideways guide (never up or down) steers each ball through the pegs toward its drawn slot. If another ball knocks it off course, the guide steers it back, and in the last few rows balls stop colliding so each one drops cleanly into its slot.

Collisions are just for show. They can't change your result. The payout always uses the slot drawn when you dropped, and the ball lands there.

No queue. Every tap, click or Space drops a ball right away, and the bet is taken then. Up to 150 balls can be on the board at once. At that cap the Drop button pauses for a moment. Drop height (Settings) only changes how far balls fall before the pegs.

Same odds every way you play. Each ball is independent, so single drops, auto-drop and button mashing all have exactly the odds shown below. If you leave while balls are falling, they're paid out from their drawn slots on your next visit.

The top prize grows with the board because edges get rarer with every row. The payouts are the same tables popular online Plinko games use. Risk spreads them differently: on Low most balls pay back around 1×, on High the middle pays 0.2× and the rare edge hits pay up to 1000×. Shuffle mixes the prizes up across the row with more frequent small wins; each slot's prize is sized to its real odds, so it pays back the same. Every board pays back the return you pick in Settings (97% unless you change it), to within 0.05 percentage points. Coins are pretend and can't be bought or cashed out.
